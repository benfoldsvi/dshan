Research

Research Directions

From transport phenomena to emerging electronic systems

Our research combines nanoscale transport, emerging magnetic materials and device engineering to develop new concepts for future electronics and computing.

Transport for Nanoelectronics

Spin, Orbital & Phonon Transport for Nanoelectronics

We investigate how spin, orbital and lattice-mediated transport can be generated, converted and controlled in nanoscale materials, with the goal of creating more efficient electronic functionalities.

  • Spin and orbital current generation and conversion
  • Spin-orbit and orbital torque devices
  • Phonon-assisted transport and angular-momentum transfer
Emerging Magnetic Materials

Antiferromagnets, Altermagnets & Emerging Materials for Spintronics

We explore new magnetic materials and unconventional symmetry-driven phenomena that can enable scalable, robust and energy-efficient spintronic devices.

  • Antiferromagnetic and altermagnetic transport
  • Epitaxial magnetic materials and heterostructures
  • Electrical detection and manipulation of magnetic order
MRAM-based Computing

MRAM, Probabilistic Computing & Process-in-Memory

We develop magnetic tunnel-junction devices and computing concepts that exploit nonvolatility, stochasticity and in-memory operation for next-generation computing architectures.

  • Spin-orbit-torque and advanced MRAM
  • Stochastic MTJs and probabilistic bits
  • Process-in-memory and neuromorphic concepts
