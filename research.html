Research

What We Study

From angular-momentum transport to electronic functionality

We investigate how spin, orbital, lattice and magnetic degrees of freedom emerge, interact and can be controlled in nanoscale materials and heterostructures. Our goal is to translate these fundamental phenomena into new concepts for memory, logic and computing.

Spin

Spin Transport & Spin-Orbit Torque

Spin generation, transport, relaxation and current-induced manipulation of magnetization in magnetic materials and heterostructures.

Spin Hall effect Spin-orbit torque Spin relaxation
Orbital

Orbital Transport & Orbitronics

Orbital Hall transport, orbital-to-spin conversion and orbital torque as new routes for efficient control of nanoscale magnetic states.

Orbital Hall effect Orbital torque Conversion
Emerging Magnetism

Antiferromagnetic & Altermagnetic Spintronics

Symmetry-driven transport, electrical detection and control of antiferromagnetic and altermagnetic order.

Antiferromagnets Altermagnets Symmetry

Phonon-Mediated Angular Momentum Transport

Lattice-mediated transport, spin-lattice coupling and phononic channels relevant to nanoscale angular-momentum transfer.
